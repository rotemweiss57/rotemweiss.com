Rotem Weiss

Rotem Weiss (Hebrew: רותם ויס; born September 23, 1996) is an Israeli technology entrepreneur, the founder and CEO of Tavily, and co-creator of the open-source project GPT Researcher.

Biography

Rotem Weiss was born in Israel in 1996 and lives in New York City. He studied computer science at Columbia University, graduating with a BA in 2024. While at Columbia he co-founded The Bulletin, a virtual bulletin board for students, which placed third in the undergraduate track of Startup Columbia 2024. Before founding Tavily he worked as a data scientist at Talenya, a Paycor company.

In 2023 Weiss co-created GPT Researcher, an open-source autonomous agent that conducts deep web research, which has become one of the most widely used open-source research agents, with about 30,000 stars on GitHub. In January 2024 he released GPT-Newspaper, an open-source agent built on LangGraph and Tavily that generates personalized newspapers. In 2024 he co-taught the DeepLearning.AI short course AI Agents in LangGraph with LangChain founder Harrison Chase.

Weiss founded Tavily in 2024; the company was incubated by Alpha Wave Global. Tavily provides web search, extraction and crawling APIs purpose-built for AI agents and large language models. Tavily raised a $5 million seed round led by Alpha Wave Global in July 2024 and a $20 million Series A co-led by Insight Partners and Alpha Wave Global in August 2025, bringing total funding to $25 million. By early 2026 the company reported more than one million developers and three million monthly SDK downloads, with customers including IBM, Cohere and Groq.

On February 10, 2026, Nebius Group (Nasdaq: NBIS) announced an agreement to acquire Tavily; the acquisition closed on February 19, 2026. Bloomberg reported the price at $275 million, and Calcalist reported a total of up to $400 million including earn-outs. Weiss continues to lead Tavily as founder and CEO within Nebius. Since 2026 he has also been a scout for Andreessen Horowitz. In 2026 Forbes Israel named him to its 30 Under 30 list.
