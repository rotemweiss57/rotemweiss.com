In brief

Rotem Weiss (born 1996) is an Israeli technology entrepreneur based in New York. He founded Tavily in 2024 and leads it today as founder and CEO within Nebius Group, which acquired the company in February 2026. He co-created the open-source project GPT Researcher, co-taught DeepLearning.AI's AI Agents in LangGraph, is a scout for Andreessen Horowitz, and was named to Forbes Israel's 30 Under 30 in 2026.
