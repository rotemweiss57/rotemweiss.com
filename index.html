In brief

Rotem Weiss is an Israeli technology entrepreneur based in New York. He founded Tavily in 2024 to connect AI agents to real-time web data. The company raised $25 million from Insight Partners and Alpha Wave Global and was acquired by Nebius Group (Nasdaq: NBIS) in February 2026 in a deal reported at $275 million upfront. He continues to lead Tavily as founder and CEO within Nebius.

Before Tavily, he co-created GPT Researcher, one of the most widely used open-source autonomous research agents, and built GPT-Newspaper. He co-taught the DeepLearning.AI course AI Agents in LangGraph with Harrison Chase. He is a scout at Andreessen Horowitz and was named to the Forbes Israel 30 Under 30 list in 2026. He holds a BA in Computer Science from Columbia University.
